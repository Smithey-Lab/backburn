numeric comes from `backburn/data/units.json`; the table below is generated from it.

Speed is cells per simulated second on cost-1.0 terrain (a cell is ~10 m, so 1.0 ≈ 36 km/h).
Cost is in scenario budget units; ETA is the dispatch delay after purchase.
"Confidence" tags where the unit came from: **documented** in the research brief,
**inferred** from screenshots/descriptions, or **new**.

## Movement classes

| Class | Can enter | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `FOOT` | grass, shrub, forest, dense forest, roads, gravel, sand, firebreak | crews and civilians |
| `ROAD` | roads, gravel; grass at 2.2× cost | engines — keep them on the pavement |
| `OFFROAD` | everything except water, dense forest and structures | brush truck, dozer |
| `AIR` | everything | straight-line flight, ignores fire |
| `WATERCRAFT` | water only | fire boat |

Ground units refuse to path through burning or smoldering cells and re-plan (with a
2 s cooldown) when a step is blocked. A ground unit standing in a cell that ignites is
**overrun**: it drops its orders and hose, retreats to the nearest safe cell, and logs
`overrun by fire, retreating`. Civilians in a burning cell are lost.

## Orders

| Order | Target | Who | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MOVE` | point | any | path there, stop |
| `SUPPRESS` | point | SPRAY units (engine, brush truck, fire boat) | move within 75% of spray radius, spray water in a disc; tank drains faster when more cells are hot; when empty, **auto-refills** at the nearest reachable water and resumes; holds when the area is cold |
| `HOSE` | point | hose team | walk there, lay a hose (A* path ≤ `hose_max_length`) to the nearest water source, spray continuously; if any hose cell burns the team stops for 8 s then reconnects |
| `CUT` | polyline | cut team, hotshots, smokejumpers, dozer | path to the first point, then walk the line converting cells to `FIREBREAK` at `cut_rate` cells/s (dozer 2 wide, can cut dense forest); stops if the next cell is burning |
| `DROP` | segment | aircraft | fly to the start, lay water/retardant along the segment (`drop_width` wide), then reload: helicopter at the nearest water (`refill_rate`), bombers back at the airbase for `reload_seconds` |
| `PICKUP` | unit id | helicopter | fly to a foot unit or civilian, take it aboard (up to `passenger_slots`) |
| `DROPOFF` | point | helicopter | unload everyone; civilians inside the **safe zone** count as rescued; crews are placed and usable (this is how smokejumpers/hotshots get to remote ground) |
| `REFILL` | point | auto | generated by the unit itself; players don't normally issue it |
| `HOLD` | — | auto | what a sprayer does when its area is cold |

`queue=True` (Shift in the viewer) appends instead of replacing. Aircraft that run dry
mid-drop go to reload and then **resume the same drop**.

## Unit states

`IDLE → MOVING → WORKING → (REFILLING | RELOADING | HOSE_BURNED) → …` plus civilian
states `FLEEING`, `SAFE`, `LOST`, and `ABOARD` for anything being carried.

## Civilians

Civilians have no orders. Each tick they look for fire within `flee_radius` cells; if
found they path to the nearest passable cell with no fire within 6 cells, preferring
away from the fire, and re-plan every 6 s. Walking into the safe zone rescues them. A
helicopter is the fast way: click a civilian while the helicopter is selected.

## Purchasing and dispatch

Scenarios may set a `budget` and an `available_units` list. `Simulation.cmd_spawn(type)`
checks both, deducts `cost`, and queues an `Arrival` that appears after `arrival_seconds`
at the **staging** point (ground) or **airbase** (aircraft). The HUD shows `incoming N`.
Scenario events can grant budget or dispatch reinforcements for free.

## Adding a unit type

1. Add an entry to `data/units.json` (copy the closest existing one). `action` must be one
   of `HOSE`, `CUT`, `SPRAY`, `DROP`, `NONE`; `movement` one of the classes above.
2. `python tools/gen_tables.py` to refresh this table.
3. If it needs new behaviour, add a handler in `units.py` and a test in `tests/`.
No other file needs to change; the viewer, buy menu, and renderer read the JSON.
